# **App Name**: PontalPark

## Core Features:

- Live Occupancy Monitor: Real-time dashboard tracking 28 parking slots with distinct UI indicators for active, free, and occupied spaces.
- Intelligent Plate Check-in: Simplified vehicle registration capturing license plate, model, and destination to automatically apply grace periods.
- Automated Fee & Grace Engine: System-driven calculations for duration and pricing based on destination-specific carência rules (Gym, Market, Pharmacy).
- Shift-Persistent Operator Session: A specialized 12-hour session management that keeps operators authenticated without requiring re-login during their plantão.
- Whitelist & Exemption Tool: Database for authorized personnel (staff, monthly members) with automated plate-check to bypass fees.
- AI Operational Insight Tool: Generative AI shift-summary tool that creates narrative performance reports and predicts peak hours based on daily logs.
- Administrative Profit Dashboard: Role-restricted access to daily/monthly billing, average ticket price, and growth charts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Modern Royal Blue (#3076F1) chosen for professional reliability and visibility in dark operational settings.
- Background color: Deep Obsidian (#14171B) to ensure high contrast and reduce eye strain for operators on night shifts.
- Accent color: Vibrant Electric Indigo (#7B71F2) for non-destructive calls-to-action and navigational highlights.
- Single font 'Inter' (Sans-serif) used throughout for its objective, machine-like clarity and high legibility on small mobile displays.
- Large, high-contrast monochrome icons for touch-friendly operations in fast-paced environments.
- Task-focused card layout with oversized buttons and immediate access to check-in/out functions.
- Snappy micro-interactions and transitions to provide tactile feedback without slowing down the operator's workflow.
